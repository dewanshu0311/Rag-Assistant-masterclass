# Final Assignment: Zyro Dynamics HR Help Desk – RAG Challenge

## Problem Statement
Managing HR queries across a large organization is time-consuming and inconsistent. Employees often struggle to get quick, accurate answers about leave policies, payroll, benefits, and compliance from HR teams.

In this assignment, you will build an AI-powered HR Help Desk chatbot that answers employee questions using Zyro Dynamics internal HR policy documents through a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline.

**Objective:** Build a complete RAG system that retrieves relevant HR policy content and generates accurate, grounded answers with guardrails to handle out-of-scope questions.

## Hosted as a Kaggle Competition
This assignment is hosted as a Kaggle InClass competition. You will work inside a Kaggle notebook, build your RAG pipeline, deploy a Streamlit chatbot, and submit your answers for evaluation on the leaderboard.

**Competition Link:** The competition link will be shared on 10 June.

### What You Need to Submit
Once your pipeline is ready, upload your `submission.csv` to the Kaggle competition it is generated automatically inside the notebook.

## How to Get Started with Kaggle
If you are new to Kaggle, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Create a Kaggle Account
- Go to [https://www.kaggle.com](https://www.kaggle.com)
- Click **Register** in the top right corner
- Sign up with your email or Google account
- Verify your email address

### Step 2: Verify Your Phone Number (Important!)
You must verify your account with a mobile number to use Internet access in notebooks. Without phone verification, you cannot call external APIs (Groq, Gemini, OpenAI) required for this project.
- Go to your Kaggle profile → Settings
- Scroll to the **Phone Verification** section
- Enter your mobile number and verify with the OTP
- Once verified, Internet access will be unlocked in notebooks

### Step 3: Join the Competition
- Open the competition link (when shared)
- Click **Join Competition**
- Accept the competition rules
- You now have access to the data, starter notebook, and submission
